# Appendix I — Evaluation ecosystem, 2026

The hundred evaluation systems — leaderboards, benchmarks, harnesses, and managed platforms — that a practitioner is most likely to meet in 2026, in priority order. The order ranks usefulness as an evaluation signal. It is an editorial judgement and does not certify quality, maintenance, or fitness for a given decision.

A score from any of these systems is a measurement under a protocol, not a property of a model. Before citing one, record the protocol fields that §61.3 and §06.6 require (DERIVED · `book_plan.md` chapters 06 and 61):

1. **System and version** — the benchmark or leaderboard release, dataset revision, and access date; refreshed benchmarks change their item pool over time.
2. **Evaluation unit** — model, checkpoint, endpoint, or complete product (§06.1); endpoint results are not model constants.
3. **Harness and scaffold** — the framework and its version, prompt template, answer extraction, tool access, and agent scaffold.
4. **Budget** — sampling settings, reasoning budget, retries, time and cost limits.
5. **Grader** — exact match, unit tests, executable verification, human raters, or a model judge (with the judge's identity and version, §62.3).
6. **Uncertainty** — number of items and runs, confidence intervals, and repeated-run consistency for agents (§63.2).
7. **Contamination status** — whether the items could have been seen in training (§08, §61.4); private and refreshed test sets exist to limit this.
