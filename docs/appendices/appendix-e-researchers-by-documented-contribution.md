## Reading an authorship relation

[DERIVED] Follow the linked spine identifier to its primary work and then the chapter's claim-level ledger. Distinguish coauthorship, stated individual contribution, prior work and later implementation. A person may participate in several areas; this table does not assign exclusive ownership of a mechanism. Preserve diacritics and disambiguate shared names using primary metadata or an author identifier rather than merging records by display name.

[DERIVED] The categories organize reading paths, not scientific priority. An algorithm can have conceptual precursors, a named formulation, a later scalable implementation and an independent replication by different groups. Cite each contribution at the appropriate boundary. A paper-specific affiliation is dated provenance; it does not establish a person's present role.

## Minimum contribution record

| Field | Required evidence |
|---|---|
| Person identity | Primary displayed name and a verified identifier where available; unresolved name collisions remain separate |
| Work and revision | Exact primary work, version and publication route; distinguish preprint and later proceedings |
| Relation | Coauthorship, explicit contribution statement or documented implementation/reproduction role |
| Mechanism | The particular estimator, objective, representation, system boundary or experiment supported by that source |
| Time | Publication/contribution date and, if relevant, paper-time affiliation; no inferred current affiliation |
| Limits | Incomplete author list, collaborative attribution, unknown individual division of work and contested priority |

[DERIVED] This appendix's initial coverage comes from the plan's named author-to-paper routes and the primary metadata recorded in Appendix D. It does not establish a comprehensive bibliometric survey or recertify every historical individual-contribution claim. New chapter-local research is credited in its full source ledger even when its authors do not appear in this compact starting index.
